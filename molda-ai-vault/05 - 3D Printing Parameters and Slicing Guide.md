# 05 - 3D Printing Parameters and Slicing Guide

To ensure that the `.STL` files generated by MoldaAí produce rigid, functional, and razor-sharp parts using additive manufacturing (FDM/FFF or Resin MSLA), this document compiles engineering parameters and slicer recommendations.

## 2. Slicer Guidelines

Meshes generated by MoldaAí are 100% compatible with domestic and industrial slicing software (**PrusaSlicer, Bambu Studio, OrcaSlicer, Ultimaker Cura, Creality Print**).

### 2.1 Nozzle Sizing & Extrusion Width
* **Standard 0.4 mm Nozzle (Most Common):**
  * A blade thickness of `0.8 mm` prints as exactly **2 continuous perimeters** (extrusion line width $0.40\text{ mm}$ to $0.45\text{ mm}$).
  * This yields a 100% solid blade wall with no internal infill gaps, maximizing cutting stiffness.
* **Fine 0.2 mm Nozzle (High-Precision Jewelry / Polymer Clay):**
  * Use `blade_thickness_mm = 0.6 mm` for 3 fine perimeters or `0.4 mm` for 2 ultra-sharp passes.

### 2.2 Layer Height
* **Initial Layer:** `0.20 mm` (promotes strong adhesion of the wide support base to the print bed).
* **Subsequent Layers:**
  * **Cookie Cutters:** `0.20 mm` to `0.24 mm` for high production speed.
  * **Clay / Ceramic Cutters:** `0.12 mm` to `0.16 mm` for minimal layer stepping and smooth cuts.

### 2.3 Perimeters and Infill
* **Wall / Perimeter Count:** Configure at least `3` perimeters in the slicer.
* **Base Infill:** $20\%$ to $40\%$ (Gyroid or Grid pattern) for the finger rest base.
* **Support Structures:** **OFF**. MoldaAí models are pre-oriented with their wide flat base resting at $Z = 0$, requiring no support material whatsoever.
* **Brim:** Generally unnecessary due to the generous contact surface provided by the base rim.

## 3. Recommended Materials & Food Safety

### 3.1 PLA Filament (Polylactic Acid)
* **Suitability:** Primary choice for cookie and polymer clay cutters.
* **Benefits:** Plant-derived, biodegradable, easy to print with zero warping, high rigidity.
* **Thermal Limit:** PLA softens at $55^\circ\text{C}$ to $60^\circ\text{C}$. **Never wash in dishwashers or expose to boiling water.**

### 3.2 PETG Filament
* **Suitability:** For cutters requiring frequent warm wash cycles and higher impact resilience.
* **Thermal Limit:** Resists hot tap water up to $70^\circ\text{C}$.

### 3.3 Food Safety Best Practices (*Food-Safe 3D Printing*)
> [!IMPORTANT]
> FDM 3D printing inherently creates microscopic layer ridges where food particles and moisture can accumulate. For commercial culinary production:
> 1. Use a dedicated food-safe stainless steel nozzle (avoid standard brass nozzles which may contain trace amounts of lead).
> 2. Coat the finished print with food-grade epoxy resin to seal layer ridges.
> 3. Hand-wash with cold or lukewarm water and mild dish soap immediately after use.
> 4. A common industry technique among pastry chefs is placing a thin sheet of plastic food wrap over the rolled dough before pressing the 3D cutter. This avoids direct plastic contact with food and facilitates clean dough release.
